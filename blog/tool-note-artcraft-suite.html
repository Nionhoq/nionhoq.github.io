ArtCraft suite — 13 Free Open-Source Creative Apps

By Nion Hoq · Nov 13, 2026 · 1 min read

ArtCraft is a suite of 13 free apps that rebuild tools people know from Adobe and Microsoft Office. PhotoCraft covers Photoshop work, FilmCraft covers Premiere work, LightCraft covers Lightroom work, and PdfCraft covers Acrobat work.

The apps run on Windows, Mac and Linux, and some run in the browser. An ArtCraft Launcher installs and updates them. They are written in Rust, built with help from Claude.

The test in my tracker was positive, not stamped. He trimmed, graded and exported video in FilmCraft, and removed a background in one click in PhotoCraft. No explicit FACT stamp was captured, so I treat this as provisional.

Cost is free. The suite is open source under Apache-2.0 and MIT, and the licence permits commercial use. Paid cloud AI generation inside the apps is optional, not required.

For me the use is Brenne Beau creative work in PhotoCraft and FilmCraft once the apps are stable, and design work for course material. It is early alpha, other testers found rough edges, so it is not for paid client work yet.

Supply-chain angle: there is no direct one. At most, PdfCraft and the spreadsheet app are general office tools, the kind of work that sits around an EDI flow, not inside it.

Link: github.com/storytold/artcraft · getartcraft.com
