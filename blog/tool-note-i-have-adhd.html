i-have-adhd — a Skill That Makes Claude Answer Short and Direct

By Nion Hoq · Nov 3, 2026 · 1 min read

i-have-adhd is a Claude Code skill that stops a coding agent from burying the answer in a long explanation.

The repo lists 10 rules. Lead with the next action. Number multi-step tasks. End with one concrete next step. Cap lists at five items. No preamble, no recap, no closers.

That changes the shape of a session. Instead of a paragraph about a file, you get the file, the line, the change to make, and the test to run next.

The verdict in my tracker is positive, not stamped. The creator tested it, said he would use it 100% of the time, and no explicit FACT stamp was captured. I treat it as provisional.

Cost: free. The repo is open source under the MIT licence, verified in its LICENSE file. You still pay your own Claude usage.

For me, the use is plain. Shorter, direct answers make Claude sessions faster to read and act on for auto-reel machine and course business dev work.

Supply-chain angle: EDI support runs on the same need. An error report that leads with the failed segment and the next step gets fixed faster than one that opens with three pages of background.

Link: github.com/ayghri/i-have-adhd
