LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.5 — a Free Talking-Avatar Model

By Nion Hoq · Oct 19, 2026 · 1 min read

LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.5 is an open-source model from Meituan's LongCat team. You give it a photo and an audio clip. It gives you back a talking video with synced lips.

The verdict from the reviewer: FACT, with a caveat. He stamped it FACT after testing, but noted quality was uneven across skin tones. I am keeping his stamp exactly as he gave it. The caveat stays on.

Cost: free. The code and model weights are open-source (MIT). The hosted demo is quota-limited, about one or two generations a day. Running it on your own machine needs a strong GPU.

This one maps to the faceless YouTube explainer idea. Avatar generation is usually the expensive step in that pipeline. A local, free model wipes it out. You still review and finish by hand. The model makes the face. You make the video.

The catch is the usual open-source trade. Real power, real friction. It needs hardware and setup time. It makes source footage. The finished video is still your job.

Link: github.com/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Video
