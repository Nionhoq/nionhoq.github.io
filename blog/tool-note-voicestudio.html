VoiceStudio — a Local Voice Cloning and Narration App

By Nion Hoq · Nov 24, 2026 · 1 min read

VoiceStudio is a free desktop app that clones a voice, designs voices, dubs video, and handles dictation, transcription, and audiobooks. It runs on your own computer, so no cloud service sits in the middle.

In his test, he cloned his own voice from a sample of about 15 seconds, and the playback sounded accurate. He gave it his FACT stamp. That stamp is his, from one test, on his own voice.

Cost is $0 for local use. The repo shows no account, API key, or subscription for the local app, and the code is AGPL-3.0. One catch matters: the default model weights are non-commercial, so paid work needs a different engine or a licence check first.

For me, the use is narration without a per-minute bill. Neon AI voiceovers, Brenne Beau reel voice, and course audio can be tested locally first. Only clone a voice you have permission to use.

Supply-chain angle: this will not fix a bad 856 map. It could read out exception summaries and training notes for warehouse staff, with the underlying documents staying on a local machine instead of going to a cloud voice service.

Link: github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio
