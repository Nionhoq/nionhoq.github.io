Bolt.new — an App Builder That Rebuilt a Typeform Form

By Nion Hoq · Nov 20, 2026 · 1 min read

Bolt.new is a browser app builder. You describe an app, it generates the code, and you publish it from the same screen.

The tip in my tracker was a rebuild job. He took a Typeform form, screenshotted only the parts he actually used, imported a Figma design, and let Bolt generate the replacement.

This was not a Fact-or-Hype test. It was a #BoltPartner post, and he gave no FACT or HYPE stamp. I treat it as a demo that worked on screen, not a tested verdict.

Cost is verified on Bolt's pricing page. Free is $0, with 300,000 tokens a day and 1 million tokens a month. Pro is $25 a month for 10 million tokens a month.

He claimed about $1,000 a year saved against Typeform. The 84 euros a month shown in the video was Typeform's plan, not Bolt's, and the post was partner-tagged, so I read the saving as a claim. For me the use is small forms and tools for course work and client work, tested on the free tier first.

Supply-chain angle: there is no EDI angle inside this tool. A supplier intake form or a delivery exception form sits around an EDI flow, and a form like that is the kind of small tool this could rebuild.
