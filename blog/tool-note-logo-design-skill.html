Logo Design Skill — a Claude Skill That Builds a Full Brand Kit

By Nion Hoq · Nov 27, 2026 · 1 min read

Logo Design Skill is a free Claude skill that turns Claude into a logo designer. It starts with a brief, checks category patterns, builds three concepts in SVG, and tests each one at small sizes and in one colour.

It also checks the concepts against a library of more than 1,400 real logos, so a new mark is less likely to copy one already in use. Pick a direction and it builds the kit: logo variations, a colour palette, favicon and app icons, guidelines, and mockups.

The creator gave it his FACT stamp. In his test he rebranded his EasyGB app and rated the result ahead of his old manual logo. The skill is free and open source under MIT. His full brand-kit run cost $3.41 in Claude usage. Some viewers called the output generic, so check the concepts before you use one in public.

For me, the use is cheap brand kits for early tests: Listic, FirstRing, and Neon AI products, plus client work where a paid logo service is hard to justify at the start. It gives a workable kit to react to, not a final brand on its own.

Supply-chain angle: there is no direct EDI angle here. The nearest use is presentation. A clean logo and clear icons help a supplier onboarding pack or a client report get read, and that is where the value stops.

Link: github.com/kaankiziltug/logo-design-skill
